Rules for your money that no one can talk around.

A CircuitWill holds your USDT0 on X Layer. You set a few simple rules. Every payment, whether you send it or an AI helper does, is checked by on-chain circuits before any money moves.

You need a wallet that supports X Layer, such as OKX Wallet or MetaMask. Viewing someone's CircuitWill doesn't need a wallet.

How it works

  1. Set your rules

    Pick a daily limit, who you trust, when you want to approve payments, and whether you can freeze. Each rule is a small circuit on X Layer.

  2. Add money

    Move USDT0 into your CircuitWill. You stay in control through a key (an NFT) that lives in your wallet.

  3. Pay with confidence

    You or your AI helper ask to pay. The circuits answer yes or no, and every answer is saved where anyone can check it.

Common questions

Who can change my rules?

Only you, the person whose wallet holds your CircuitWill's key. Other people can connect their wallets and create their own CircuitWill, but they can't touch yours. Changes apply from the very next payment.

What is the key?

When you create a CircuitWill, a key (an NFT called CircuitWillKey) is sent to your wallet. Whoever holds the key controls the will. Keep it in your wallet; sending it to someone else hands them control.

What can an AI helper do?

If you add one, an AI helper can ask to send payments, and nothing else. It can't change rules, approve payments or withdraw, and the circuits check its requests exactly like yours.

What happens when a payment is blocked?

No money moves. The page tells you which rule said no and why, for example "this would go over today's limit". The answer is final and saved on X Layer.

Can the rules themselves be changed?

What each rule checks is fixed in its circuit and can't be edited. The numbers it uses, like your daily limit, are yours to change at any time.
